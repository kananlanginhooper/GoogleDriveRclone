Privacy policy — GoogleDriveRclone

Last updated 2026-09-27.

GoogleDriveRclone is a personal, single-user tool. It is not a public service, and it is not offered to anyone besides its owner. This page exists to meet Google's OAuth app requirements.

What data it accesses

The app requests only the drive.file Google Drive scope. Under that scope, Google Drive limits it to files and folders the app itself creates — it cannot see, read, or change any other file in the account's Drive.

What it does with that access

It uploads a one-way copy of files from the owner's own home server into a folder the app created in the owner's own Google Drive, as an offsite backup. It reads back file checksums to confirm each upload matches the original. It does not read, download, or alter any other content in the Drive account.

What is not done

  • No data is sold, shared, or shown to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising.
  • No human other than the account owner reviews the uploaded content.
  • Nothing is collected outside of the files the owner chooses to back up.

Data retention

Uploaded files stay in the owner's own Google Drive under the owner's control, and can be deleted by the owner at any time through Drive itself. The app keeps no separate copy or log of file contents; it keeps only a local record (on the owner's own server) of which files it has already uploaded, to avoid re-uploading them.
